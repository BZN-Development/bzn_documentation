The look of a character: the creator a new character goes through before its first spawn,
and (in later versions) the clothing shop, barber, surgeon and wardrobe.

Repository: [BZN-Development/bzn_appearance](https://github.com/BZN-Development/bzn_appearance)

```ini
ensure bzn_core
ensure ox_inventory
ensure bzn_appearance
ensure bzn_multichar
```

## Clothing that never moves [#clothing-that-never-moves]

GTA numbers clothing in one long list across every pack. Add a pack and the numbers after
it shift, so outfits saved by number turn into other clothes. bzn_appearance saves clothing
by **collection** (the pack's name, `''` for the base game) and the drawable's **local
index** inside it, using FiveM's collection natives:

```json
{ "11": { "c": "police_pack", "d": 12, "t": 2 } }
```

Adding packs never changes that. Two rules keep it true:

1. Add new clothing as a new pack, or at the **end** of an existing one. Never insert in
   the middle: that shifts the local indices after it.
2. Do not rename a pack. If you move or merge clothing yourself, add a remap (below).

Clothing that is gone (a removed pack) is skipped and the base item is worn instead; the
saved look is kept, so it comes back with the pack.

## The creator [#the-creator]

A character without a saved look (new, or made before bzn_appearance was installed) is held
back before its first spawn: it gets the creator in GTA Online's creator room, alone in its
own routing bucket. When it saves, it spawns as usual. Turn this off with
`Config.requireCreator = false`.

## Shops [#shops]

`Config.shops` places the shops (blip per type, and `[E]` when a player stands at one). A
shop is the editor with only its own tabs. Everything goes in a **cart** (the bag at the top
right) and is paid once, in cash or from the bank (bzn_core's `accounts.pay`). With
`payTo = '<group>'` the money goes to that group's account; otherwise it leaves the economy.
The server works the bill out itself, so a client cannot change what it pays.

**Clothing shops** (`type = 'clothing'`): what the player wears and does not own is in the
cart; **+ Cart** keeps a piece in it while they try the next one on (e.g. one top in three
colours, up to `Config.maxCart`). After paying, everything in the cart is owned. Price of a
piece, in order:

1. a price set on it with `/clothing_set` (for one colour, or the whole piece),
2. its slot's price in `Config.prices`, times its pack's multiplier in `Config.prices.collections`.

Owned clothing is free to wear again (`Config.ownedFree`). Hidden clothing
(`/clothing_set <slot> hidden on`) is skipped in the creator and the shops and cannot be
newly put on; staff in `/appearance` see everything.

**Barbers** (`type = 'barber'`): hair, beard, eyebrows, makeup and the other overlays. The
price is per change (`Config.barberPrices`): a new hairstyle, new hair colours, and each
overlay that changed.

## Outfits and wardrobes [#outfits-and-wardrobes]

A character can save what it wears as an outfit (the **Outfits** tab in clothing shops and
wardrobes, up to `Config.maxOutfits`). **Share** gives an outfit a code like `BZN-7KQ2X`;
another player types it in to try it on. In a clothing shop the pieces they do not own go in
the cart; in a wardrobe they need to own everything (`Config.wardrobe = 'owned'`) or not
(`'all'`). The owner can stop sharing at any time.

A **wardrobe** changes clothing for free. Put them in `Config.wardrobes`, or open one from
your own script (e.g. housing):

```lua
exports.bzn_appearance:openWardrobe(src)
```

## Photos [#photos]

The shops and the creator show a photo of each piece of clothing and a photo grid per slot.
The photos are made by the staff tool **bzn_photos** (start it only when you take photos) and
saved in `thumbnails/` as WebP. bzn_appearance serves them over the server's own HTTP
endpoint (`http://<server>/bzn_appearance/thumb/<file>.webp`), so a player only downloads the
photos they look at, and new photos work without a restart. Clothing without a photo shows
its number.

## The clothing menu [#the-clothing-menu]

Players take pieces off and put them back on, and wear a piece its other way: a jacket
open or closed, a hood up or down, a visor up or down, trousers in or over the boots, hair
put up. Each has a short animation. The default key is `Y` (players can rebind it), and
the menu is a wheel of its own from `bzn_interface`: beside the middle of the screen, and
open until the player closes it. It has a wheel per part of the body.

Nothing in it is saved. The saved look is what the character owns; the menu only decides
how much of it shows right now. Whatever dresses the character again (a spawn,
`/reloadskin`, the editor) puts everything back, and so does "Put all on".

A piece the character is not wearing, or that has no other way to be worn, is shown faint
and cannot be chosen.

| Group | Pieces |
| --- | --- |
| Head | hat, visor, mask, glasses, earpiece, hair up / down |
| Upper body | jacket open / hood, top, undershirt, undershirt in / out, vest |
| Legs | trousers, trousers in / over, shoes, shoes in / over |
| Accessories | neck, neck in / out, bag, bag open / closed, gloves, watch, bracelet |

Settings are in `Config.toggle`: the key, where the wheel sits, animations, whether it
works in a vehicle, and `bare`: what each slot wears when its piece is off (per sex).

### Where the other ways come from [#where-the-other-ways-come-from]

`catalog/toggles_<model>.json`, made by `tools/clothing-indexer`:

```
node toggles.mjs
```

It reads the game's own list of variants per piece (so every jacket, hood, boot and chain
Rockstar made switchable is in it), adds hand-collected pairs for what the game data does
not hold (visors, bags, hair: `toggle-pairs.json`), and writes everything as collection +
number, like saved looks. Run it again after a game update that adds clothing.

Gloves are part of the arms, and taking a top off needs other arms: both come from
`catalog/arms_<model>.json` (`node arms.mjs`), the same file the editor uses.

Add-on packs have no variants unless you add them: put the pairs in the `rings` of the
toggles file by hand, as `"<pack>|<number>": "<pack>|<number>"` both ways.

## Commands [#commands]

| Command | Who | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/reloadskin` | everyone | a fresh ped with the saved look; health and armour are kept (cooldown `Config.reloadCooldown`, not in vehicles, combat, water or while falling) |
| `/appearance [id]` | `group.admin` | opens the free editor for a player (default: yourself) |
| `/appearance_scan` | `group.admin` | reads every clothing pack on your client and stores it as the catalog |
| `/clothing_info` | `group.admin` | pack, drawable, texture, price and shop data of everything you wear (chat and F8) |
| `/clothing_set <slot> <price\|label\|hidden\|section> <value> [texture]` | `group.admin` | shop data of what you wear in a slot (`11`, or `p0` for props); `clear` removes a price, label or section; `texture` sets only the colour you wear |
| `/appearance_tags` | `group.admin` | reads the clothing tag files in `catalog/` again after a new export, without a restart |
| `/appearance_remap model kind slot fromC fromD toC toD [textureOffset]` | `group.admin` | clothing moved to another pack; `-` is the base game |

Examples:

```
/appearance 12                       open the free editor for player 12
/clothing_set 11 price 450           the jacket you wear costs 450, in every colour
/clothing_set 11 price 600 texture   only the colour you wear costs 600
/clothing_set 11 label Leather jacket
/clothing_set p0 hidden on           the hat you wear is not sold and not in the creator
/clothing_set 10 section 1           show what you wear in slot 10 under section 1 in the shop
/clothing_set 11 price clear         back to the default price
/appearance_remap mp_m_freemode_01 component 11 police_pack 12 police_pack_v2 27 0
```

Run `/appearance_scan` after adding or removing clothing packs. It compares with the last scan
and warns staff (chat, F8 and the server console) when a pack lost pieces or a piece's colour
count changed: that means pieces moved inside the pack, and saved looks may now show the
wrong clothing. Fix it with `/appearance_remap`, or keep the old version of the pack. Until a model is scanned,
saved clothing is only checked for its format, not whether it exists.

## API [#api]

Server:

```lua
exports.bzn_appearance:get(charId)        -- saved look or nil
exports.bzn_appearance:openEditor(src)    -- free editor (staff tools)
AddEventHandler('bzn_appearance:saved', function(src, charId, data) end)
```

Client:

```lua
exports.bzn_appearance:apply(ped, data)   -- dress any ped (no waits)
exports.bzn_appearance:getOwn()           -- the player's saved look

-- The clothing menu. Piece ids: hat, visor, mask, glasses, ear, hair, jacket, shirt,
-- undershirt, undershirt_style, vest, pants, pants_style, shoes, shoes_style, neck,
-- neck_style, bag, bag_style, gloves, watch, bracelet.
exports.bzn_appearance:toggleClothing(id) -- off / on, or the other way to wear it
exports.bzn_appearance:resetClothing()    -- everything back as saved
exports.bzn_appearance:isClothingOff(id)  -- true while the piece is off
AddEventHandler('bzn_appearance:toggled', function(id) end)  -- id, or 'reset'

-- `/reloadskin` gives the player a fresh ped (health, max health and armour are kept, so it
-- never heals). Set your own ped state again here (walk style, flags):
AddEventHandler('bzn_appearance:pedChanged', function(ped) end)
```

## Uses from bzn_core [#uses-from-bzn_core]

- `spawn:beforeSpawn` holds the spawn back for the creator, `spawn.resume` lets it go on.
- `spawn:pedReady` dresses the ped before the fade-in.
- `characters.registerPreview('appearance', …)` gives selectors (bzn_multichar) each
  character's look.
