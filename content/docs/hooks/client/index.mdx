Every hook bzn fires on the client, one page per area. The tables below list all of them:
click a hook for what it means and an example.

How to listen is on the [Hooks](/docs/hooks) page.

## What client hooks are for [#what-client-hooks-are-for]

Client hooks are about **the local player only**: their character, their groups, their money, their statuses. They are how a HUD, a phone or a menu stays up to date without asking the server.

```lua
bzn.hooks.on('groups:dutyChanged', function(duty, previous)
    SendNUIMessage({ action = 'duty', group = duty })
end)
```

- There are no before-hooks on the client: nothing here can stop an action. Rules belong on the server.
- What a client knows is a copy. The server checks everything again, so never trust it for a decision that matters.
