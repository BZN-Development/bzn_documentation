## `role:changed` [#role-changed]

`(role)` The local player's staff role changed (`nil` for none). Use it to show or hide
admin UI.

```lua
bzn.hooks.on('role:changed', function(role)
    SendNUIMessage({ action = 'staff', visible = role ~= nil })
end)
```
